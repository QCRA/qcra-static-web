Contact QCRA

Get in touch with the Queensfield Court Residents’ Association.

Membership enquiries

If you would like to join the Association, or have a question about membership, please contact:

join@qcra.org.uk

If you submit a membership application, the Association will contact you separately about the subscription payment arrangements.

General enquiries

For general correspondence, questions or matters you would like to bring to the Association’s attention, please contact the secretary:

secretary@qcra.org.uk

Preparing a query

A concise description of the matter, the block or area affected, relevant dates, and references to supporting documents will help the Association understand it.

For building issues, distinguish what you directly observed from what you suspect or would like clarified. Avoid including unnecessary personal information.
