Contact QCRA

Ways to get in touch with the Residents’ Association.

Contact arrangements

The association’s contact arrangements are being finalised. This page will be updated when a contact address and the preferred method for raising an issue are ready to publish.

Please do not assume that a contact address or submission form is available until it is listed here.

If you are preparing a query

A concise description of the matter, the block or area affected, relevant dates, and references to supporting documents will help the association understand it.

For building issues, distinguish what you directly observed from what you suspect or would like clarified. Avoid including unnecessary personal information.
