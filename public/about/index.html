About QCRA

A collective voice for Queensfield Court.

Our purpose

Queensfield Court Residents’ Association (QCRA) provides a shared, independent way for residents to discuss matters affecting the Court and raise common concerns constructively.

We aim to represent residents’ concerns clearly, keep useful records, and communicate on the basis of evidence.

Our principles

QCRA aims to be constructive, independent, open, fair and democratic. We seek to distinguish documented facts from questions or concerns that still need investigation.

Who can take part?

The association has arrangements for full membership by leaseholders and associate participation by tenants and other residents. The Join QCRA page explains the current membership approach.

How we work

The association can help residents bring related concerns together, maintain a record of issues, share relevant documents, and communicate with the property manager where appropriate.

For current updates, see What’s happening. For longer-term reference material, visit Information.
