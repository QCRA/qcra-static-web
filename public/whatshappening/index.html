Queensfield Court Residents’ Association launches

November 2026

Queensfield Court Residents’ Association (QCRA) is launching this month. The Association is being established to give leaseholders, residents and others with an interest in Queensfield Court an independent, organised way to work together on matters affecting the Court.

By bringing people together, we can share information and experience, identify common concerns, discuss issues affecting our homes and community, and help shape the Association’s priorities for the future.

Members of the committee plan to begin visiting residents over the weekend of 7 November and 8 November 2026 to introduce QCRA, explain what it is intended to do and invite people to get involved.

You do not need to commit to attending every meeting to benefit from being involved. Membership provides an opportunity to keep informed, share your views and contribute to matters that affect Queensfield Court.

Find out more about the Association and the membership arrangements on the Join QCRA page.
