Queensfield Court Residents’ Association

A collective voice for Queensfield Court.

QCRA brings together people who own and live at Queensfield Court to share information, raise common concerns and work constructively on matters affecting our homes and community.

New members are welcome

We are building an independent residents’ association that gives people at Queensfield Court an opportunity to have their say and help shape its future.

Whether you are a leaseholder or a resident, joining QCRA is a practical way to keep informed, share your experience and contribute to issues that affect the Court. You do not need to attend every meeting to make your membership worthwhile.
