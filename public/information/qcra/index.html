The Residents’ Association

How QCRA is intended to represent residents and work constructively.

Purpose

Queensfield Court Residents’ Association (QCRA) provides a shared, independent way for residents to discuss matters affecting the Court and raise common concerns constructively.

Principles

QCRA aims to be constructive, independent, open, fair and democratic. Its records should distinguish documented facts from concerns, questions and matters that remain under investigation.

Membership and participation

Full membership is intended for leaseholders, with associate participation available for tenants and other residents. See Join QCRA for the current working approach; registration and subscription arrangements are still being finalised.

How the association works

The association can bring related concerns together, maintain an evidence-based record, share relevant documents and communicate with the property manager where appropriate. What’s happening carries current updates.
