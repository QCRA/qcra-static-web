Associate membership

Associate membership is free. The supplied form describes membership for residents and others who have an interest in matters affecting Queensfield Court.

What associate membership offers

Associate members can take part in QCRA and raise and discuss matters affecting residents, including their home, garages, parking, communal areas, repairs, maintenance and the wider management of Queensfield Court.

How to apply

Complete the Associate Membership & Authority Form and return it to the QCRA Secretary at flat 3, Queensfield Court. The form asks for your contact details and signature, and authorises QCRA to make representations on matters affecting Queensfield Court within the scope described in the form.
