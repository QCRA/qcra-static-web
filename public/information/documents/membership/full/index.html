Full membership

Welcome to the Queensfield Court Residents’ Association. This page explains full membership and provides the current form for committee review.

Membership form status: not ready for applications

The supplied form still contains placeholders for the annual subscription and bank details. Please do not submit payment or rely on this form until QCRA publishes a completed version.

Who can join?

Full membership is intended for a lessee of a flat at Queensfield Court. There is one full membership for each flat. Where a flat has joint lessees, at least one lessee must hold the paid full membership.

How to apply

When the form has been finalised, complete the membership and authority form, including the lessee details and signature(s), and return it to the QCRA Secretary at flat 3, Queensfield Court. The form also records the authority for QCRA to make representations on matters affecting Queensfield Court within its stated scope.

Welcome information for landlords and non-resident owners

QCRA welcomes people who have an interest in Queensfield Court, including owners who do not live here. The welcome document explains why their involvement matters, the issues the Association can help owners consider collectively, and ways to participate without attending every meeting.
