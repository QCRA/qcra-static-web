Join QCRA

Help build a collective voice for Queensfield Court.

Full membership

Full membership is for leaseholders. The association’s working membership model is one full membership per flat; the membership form will explain the subscription and registration arrangements, including where a flat has joint leaseholders.

Associate membership

Associate membership is intended for tenants and other residents who want to participate in discussion of matters affecting them. These may include maintenance, parking, garages, trees and other shared concerns.

Associate membership is unpaid and does not replace the full-membership arrangements for leaseholders.

How to join

The membership form and accompanying instructions are being prepared and will be published here when ready. They will explain what information is needed and how to submit an application.

If you have a question in the meantime, use the Contact page.

Questions about membership

For the association’s purpose and approach, see About QCRA.
