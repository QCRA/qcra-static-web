Join QCRA

Queensfield Court works better when we work together.

QCRA is being built to give the people who own and live at Queensfield Court an independent, organised way to share information, raise concerns and have a collective voice on matters affecting our homes and our community.

Why join?

Many of the issues affecting Queensfield Court are shared by more than one household. By working together, we can understand those issues better, identify common concerns and make our views heard more effectively.

A collective voice

When leaseholders and residents share their experiences, individual concerns can become a clearer picture of wider problems. An organised association gives us a way to raise common issues collectively with the managing agent and other relevant organisations.

Better information

Sharing information helps us understand what is happening around the Court, compare experiences and establish the facts behind decisions about maintenance, management, expenditure and communal areas.

A say in what happens next

QCRA should reflect the concerns and priorities of the people it represents. Membership gives you an opportunity to raise matters, contribute ideas and help decide what the Association should work on.

An organisation for the long term

We want to build an independent, open and democratic association that can work constructively on issues affecting Queensfield Court, rather than simply reacting to individual problems as they arise.

One of our longer-term aims is to seek recognition as a Residents’ Association under the Landlord and Tenant Act 1985. Formal recognition can give a qualifying association important rights concerning information, service charges and consultation.

Get involved in a way that suits you

You do not need to attend every meeting or take on a committee role to make membership worthwhile. You can keep informed, share your experience, raise an issue when it matters to you or become more involved as time permits.

Membership

QCRA has two membership categories, so that both leaseholders and other residents can have a connection with the Association.

Full membership

Full membership is intended for leaseholders. It provides the principal membership of the Association and the basis for its formal membership decisions, subject to the Constitution.

The working membership model is one full membership per flat. The membership arrangements will explain how registration works where a flat has joint leaseholders.

Associate membership

Associate membership is intended for tenants and other residents who have an interest in Queensfield Court and want to contribute to discussion about matters affecting the community.

This may include maintenance, parking, garages, trees, communal areas and other shared concerns. Associate membership is unpaid and does not replace the full-membership arrangements for leaseholders.

How to join

We welcome applications from leaseholders and other residents of Queensfield Court. Please use the appropriate membership page to find out more and download the application form.

Leaseholders

For full membership, including the membership application and authority form, visit Full membership .

Tenants and other residents

For associate membership, including the application and authority form, visit Associate membership .

Payment is not required when submitting an application. The Association will contact applicants separately about subscription payment arrangements, where applicable.

If you have any questions about membership or the application process, please contact join@qcra.org.uk.

Find out more

You can read more about the Association, its purpose and how it intends to operate on the About QCRA page. Updates about the launch, meetings and other developments will appear on What’s happening.
